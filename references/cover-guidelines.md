# Cover Guidelines

Use this for 视频号, 小红书, 抖音, B站, article thumbnails, and campaign cover recommendations.

## Cover Job

A cover should answer one question quickly: "Why should this audience stop here?"

It should communicate:

- Audience or scene.
- Pain point, promise, contradiction, or question.
- Content type: case, list, interview, warning, demo, comparison, trend.
- Creator credibility or brand relevance when useful.

## AI Medical Cover Angles

Useful directions:

- Scene pain: "医生每天被文书拖住多久?"
- Workflow contrast: "AI进病房前后, 差别在哪?"
- Decision question: "医院该先上哪类AI?"
- Trust boundary: "AI能辅助, 但不能替你看病"
- Trend signal: "医疗AI从炫技走向落地"
- Case curiosity: "一个随访场景, 为什么适合AI?"
- Professional warning: "医疗AI宣传最容易踩的坑"

## Visual Guidance

- Use one dominant subject: KOL face, doctor scene, product screen, hospital workflow, patient service moment, event site, or large text question.
- Keep cover text short: 6-14 Chinese characters is often easier to scan.
- Avoid dense slogans, long product names, and multiple logos competing for attention.
- Use contrast between human and AI/workflow when the content explains technology adoption.
- For professional medical content, prioritize credibility and clarity over exaggerated shock.

## Cover Text Formula Bank

- 痛点型: `[人群]最头疼的[问题]`
- 反常识型: `[常见判断]可能错了`
- 方法型: `[场景]的[数字]个判断`
- 趋势型: `[行业变化]正在发生`
- 边界型: `[AI能做什么]和[不能做什么]`
- 案例型: `一个[场景], 看懂[趋势/价值]`
- 对比型: `[过去做法] vs [AI辅助后]`

## Review Checklist

- Is the text readable on mobile?
- Does the first frame support the cover promise?
- Is the title different from but complementary to the cover?
- Does it avoid medical overclaiming?
- Would the KOL normally publish something with this visual tone?
