# Review Checklist

Use this file for titles, scripts, captions, covers, KOL drafts, and campaign plans.

## Strategic Fit

- Does the content serve the campaign objective: awareness, trust, education, lead generation, launch, event, recruitment, or reputation?
- Is the target audience specific enough?
- Does the content solve a user problem instead of only introducing the brand?
- Is the creator's role clear: witness, explainer, expert, user, host, interviewer, or commentator?
- Does the CTA match the audience's readiness?

## Content Quality

- Hook appears in the first 3 seconds or first sentence.
- One main idea is dominant; secondary points do not dilute it.
- There is a concrete scene, user pain, story, data point, comparison, or example.
- The script has movement: problem -> insight -> proof -> implication -> action.
- The language sounds like the KOL, not a press release.
- Jargon is translated into audience-relevant meaning.

## AI Medical Specificity

- AI capability is explained through a real workflow or decision moment.
- The value is tied to efficiency, access, accuracy support, experience, cost, training, or coordination without overstating certainty.
- Human professional responsibility remains clear.
- Claims avoid implying diagnosis, treatment, prevention, cure, guaranteed outcomes, or replacement of clinicians unless formally approved.

## Platform Fit

- Title creates curiosity or utility without clickbait.
- Cover communicates one clear reason to watch.
- First frame and title are aligned.
- Script length matches the platform and creator's usual rhythm.
- Comment prompt is natural and not forced.

## Multi-KOL Differentiation

Check that KOLs are not repeating the same brief:

- Different audience segment.
- Different use case or scene.
- Different emotional angle.
- Different proof type.
- Different CTA or next step.
- Different creator-native format.

## Review Labels

Use these labels:

- `Must fix`: factual, compliance, brand, or audience-fit issue.
- `Should improve`: clarity, pacing, hook, proof, or creator-fit issue.
- `Optional polish`: wording, rhythm, visual emphasis, title variation.

## Final Review Output

For each draft, give:

- Overall judgment.
- Strongest part.
- Biggest risk.
- Must-fix revisions.
- Suggested rewrite or direction.
- Compliance notes.
- Approval status: approve, approve with edits, revise and resubmit, or reject.
