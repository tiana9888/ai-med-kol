# AI Medical And Health Communication Guardrails

This is a planning aid, not legal, regulatory, or medical advice. For regulated campaigns, state what brand, legal, medical, or compliance reviewers must confirm.

## High-Risk Claims

Flag and soften claims that imply:

- Guaranteed diagnosis, treatment, prevention, cure, survival, recovery, or health outcome.
- Replacement of doctors, nurses, hospitals, pharmacists, or clinical judgment.
- Universal applicability across patients, hospitals, diseases, regions, or devices.
- Official endorsement, certification, clinical validation, or regulatory approval without evidence.
- Patient data use without consent, privacy protection, or security explanation.
- Before/after medical results that may be interpreted as proof of efficacy.

## Safer Framing

Prefer:

- "辅助", "支持", "帮助提升", "用于某一流程", "在特定场景下", "供专业人员参考".
- Workflow value: triage support, documentation efficiency, follow-up management, patient education, scheduling, quality control, training.
- Evidence boundaries: "基于品牌提供资料", "在某试点场景", "仍需医生判断", "具体效果取决于实际部署".
- User education: explain what the tool can and cannot do.

Avoid:

- "替代医生", "百分百准确", "治愈", "根治", "立刻见效", "最先进", "唯一", "零风险", "保证".

## Patient And Case Stories

Check:

- Consent and privacy are explicit when real patient material is used.
- Identifiable details are removed unless approved.
- Stories do not imply every patient will get the same outcome.
- Emotional storytelling does not exploit fear, shame, or urgency.
- Patient-facing advice includes "consult a qualified professional" where needed.

## Expert And KOL Boundaries

For doctor or medical professional KOLs:

- Keep statements within the expert's qualification and evidence.
- Avoid personalized diagnosis or treatment instructions in public content.
- Distinguish education from medical advice.
- Avoid using professional identity to endorse unverified product efficacy.

For non-medical KOLs:

- Do not let them present clinical conclusions as expertise.
- Position them as experiencers, interviewers, observers, or translators of provided information.

## Data And AI Transparency

Look for:

- What data is used or not used.
- Whether outputs are recommendations, summaries, alerts, or administrative support.
- Human review points in the workflow.
- Privacy, security, and compliance references when relevant.
- Known limitations or appropriate-use boundaries.

## Review Response Pattern

When risk appears, answer with:

- Risk sentence: what could be interpreted incorrectly.
- Safer direction: how to reframe.
- Suggested rewrite.
- Reviewer dependency: what brand/legal/medical team should verify.
