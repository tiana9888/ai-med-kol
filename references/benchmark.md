# Benchmark Research And KOL Account Analysis

Use this file before writing briefs, scripts, title banks, or multi-KOL plans.

## Research Scope

Collect patterns from:

- Direct competitors in AI healthcare, digital health, hospital technology, clinical workflow, medical imaging, pharma tech, health management, or patient service.
- Adjacent categories: SaaS productivity, medical education, doctor IP, hospital operations, elderly care, chronic disease management, insurance, consumer health.
- Platform-native winners: accounts with strong completion, comment, save, share, or lead-generation behavior even outside healthcare.

If live web access is available and the user asks for current examples, verify with search. If not, state that benchmark conclusions are based on provided material or general platform logic.

## Benchmark Dimensions

Analyze each case through these lenses:

- Account positioning: expert, founder, doctor, operator, patient companion, industry observer, product educator, media account.
- Audience: hospital decision makers, doctors, nurses, patients/families, investors, channel partners, health-conscious consumers, policy/industry readers.
- Content job: awareness, trust building, education, lead capture, event warm-up, product demo, objection handling, employer branding.
- Hook: pain point, data, contrarian view, story conflict, scenario question, professional warning, before/after workflow, policy trend.
- Proof: case, demo, expert identity, research data, workflow comparison, customer quote, patient journey, product screen, third-party report.
- Format: talking-head, interview, field visit, case breakdown, listicle, screen demo, dialogue, myth-busting, vlog, live event recap.
- Engagement trigger: asks for experience, invites comment, offers checklist, challenges misconception, names a common anxiety, gives a practical decision rule.
- Conversion path: profile visit, consultation, whitepaper, event sign-up, product trial, private message, sales handoff.

## KOL Account Analysis

For each KOL, summarize:

- Native persona and audience trust source.
- Best-performing topics and repeatable structures.
- Typical opening style, pacing, visual language, title formula, cover pattern, CTA style.
- Fit with brand: audience overlap, credibility match, product explanation ability, risk tolerance.
- Risk: overclaiming, medical advice tone, inconsistent values, poor comment quality, low originality, excessive advertising feel.
- Recommended collaboration mode: seeded topic, co-created script, interview, event attendance, product experience, case explanation, expert reaction, series.

## Opportunity Synthesis

Do not stop at listing examples. Turn research into choices:

- Which angles are saturated?
- Which audience anxieties are under-addressed?
- Which proof types are missing in competitor content?
- Which creator styles can make the brand more credible or more approachable?
- Which platform-native formats should be adapted rather than copied?

## Output Pattern

Use this concise structure:

1. Benchmark summary: 3-5 useful patterns.
2. KOL fit judgment: who is best for what job.
3. Differentiation opportunities: angle, format, proof, CTA.
4. Risks and guardrails.
5. Recommended content directions.
