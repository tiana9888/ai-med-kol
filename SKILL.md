# AI医疗KOL内容策划 Skill

Use this skill to support AI医疗/健康科技 brands across KOL content planning, account analysis, briefs, titles, scripts, draft review, cover direction, compliance checks, and multi-KOL differentiation.

## Core Workflow

1. Clarify the brand context, campaign objective, target audience, product claims, required CTAs, platform, and regulatory sensitivity. If context is missing, use `references/brand-context-template.md`.
2. Run benchmark thinking before creating: compare topic angles, creator positioning, hook styles, content formats, comments, covers, and conversion paths. Use `references/benchmark.md`.
3. Analyze each KOL account: audience fit, credibility, content rhythm, strongest recurring formats, risk points, and what the brand should or should not ask them to do.
4. Produce or refine the deliverable: KOL brief, title bank, short-video script, talking points, comment guidance, cover recommendation, or review notes. Use templates when helpful.
5. Apply AI医疗/健康 compliance review before finalizing. Use `references/medical-compliance.md` for claims, disclaimers, patient stories, professional boundaries, and risk language.
6. If multiple KOLs are involved, make each assignment differentiated by audience, scene, story angle, proof type, and emotional tone instead of repeating the same talking points.
7. End with concrete next actions: what to revise, what to approve, what to test, and what brand/legal/medical reviewers must confirm.

## Reference Map

- `references/benchmark.md`: full-process benchmark research and KOL account analysis.
- `references/review-checklist.md`: script, draft, title, caption, and campaign review criteria.
- `references/medical-compliance.md`: AI medical/health communication risk guardrails.
- `references/cover-guidelines.md`: short-video and article cover recommendations.
- `references/brand-context-template.md`: information to collect before strategy work.

## Templates

- `templates/kol-profile.md`: reusable KOL account analysis profile.
- `templates/brief-template.md`: creator brief structure.
- `templates/review-scorecard.md`: scored draft review format.

## Operating Rules

- Prefer Chinese output when the user works in Chinese or the campaign is China-facing.
- Treat benchmark research as strategy input, not copying. Extract patterns, gaps, and opportunities.
- Separate creator authenticity from brand control: preserve the KOL's native style while locking the facts, claims, and CTA.
- For medical or health content, flag uncertainty and reviewer dependencies instead of over-certifying compliance.
- When judging content, distinguish `must fix`, `should improve`, and `optional polish`.
